The bundled gds3d-cjk.otf is a FontTools subset containing the
common Simplified Chinese characters in the GB2312 byte range plus the
Chinese characters used by shrieker. Latin glyphs are intentionally omitted;
egui's default fonts render Latin text before falling back to this font.
The modified font's internal family and PostScript names are "Shrieker CJK"
and "ShriekerCJK-Regular" to comply with the Reserved Font Name requirement.
